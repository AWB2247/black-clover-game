# Black Clover Saga

A fan-made side-scrolling action game. Play Asta through the **Anime Saga** (colour, episodes),
then turn the page into the **Manga Saga** (ink and screentone, chapters).

No build step and no dependencies: open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls
| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or arrow keys |
| Jump | W, Up or Space |
| Slash (anti-magic: reflects enemy bolts) | J or Z |
| Black Meteorite / Hurricane / Divider | K or X (25 mana) |
| Dash (brief invulnerability) | Shift or L |
| Black Form (when the rage bar is full) | Q |
| Call a Black Bull | 1 Noelle, 2 Yami, 3 Luck, 4 Charmy, 5 Yuno |
| Pause / sound | P / M |

Touch buttons appear automatically on phones and tablets.

## Structure
- **Anime Saga** (5 episodes): Hage Village, the Black Bull hideout, the Witches' Forest,
  the Royal Knights Selection Exam, and the Elf Reincarnation arc, with Demon-Slayer sword.
- **Manga Saga** (3 chapters): the Spade Kingdom raid, the siege of the capital and the final
  battle, with Demon-Dweller then Demon-Destroyer swords.
- Progress unlocks Stage Select (saved in `localStorage`).

Fan-made tribute to *Black Clover* by Yuki Tabata. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the creator,
Shueisha, TV Tokyo or Studio Pierrot. Story beats are loosely adapted, not a faithful retelling.
