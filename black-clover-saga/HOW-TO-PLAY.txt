BLACK CLOVER SAGA
=================
Double-click play-me.html (works on its own), or open index.html (needs game.js beside it).
Use any modern browser. Needs internet only for the nice fonts; the game works offline.

Keys: A/D move, W/Space jump, J slash, K special, Shift dash, Q Black Form, 1-5 call allies, P pause, M sound.
Phone/tablet: on-screen buttons appear automatically.
